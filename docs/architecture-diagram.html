AI_news: architecture

A daily AI-industry brief that runs on GCP and posts to Telegram, plus job-market tracking and a career-fit report. Draft v0.2 · 2026-10-12.

Daily flow

Scheduler fires the job. The job pulls the SQLite snapshot from Cloud Storage, runs steps 1–6, merges Telegram feedback from Firestore, and uploads the snapshot again with a generation lock.

Jobs and career fit

Job boards are polled every day. Haiku extracts skills, stack and salary. Each week the skill trends are compared with the private skills inventory, and the result goes into the Monday market-fit report.

Feedback loop

Button clicks reach the webhook and are stored in Firestore. Each run merges them into the scores, and once a week the profile is rewritten from them for you to review.

Cost

Infrastructure is about $0–1/mo on free tiers. LLM tokens are about $15–35/mo, mostly the Opus brief writer.
